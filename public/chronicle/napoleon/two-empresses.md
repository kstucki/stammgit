That [[p:napoleon_i_bonaparte]] was married twice is visible in the
tree at a glance: the marriage to [[p:josephine_de_beauharnais]] as a
shared box, the second to [[p:marie_louise_of_austria]] as a ring line.
What the tree does not tell is the why.

Josephine brought two children from her first marriage — but the
marriage to Napoleon remained childless, and an empire without an heir
was unthinkable to Napoleon. In 1810 he had the marriage annulled and
married the eighteen-year-old archduchess Marie Louise; only a year
later [[p:napoleon_ii_bonaparte]] was born, who hangs from the ring
line in the tree.

The sources for both marriages: [[s:/sources/wikipedia-napoleon-i.pdf]]
and [[s:/sources/wikipedia-josephine-de-beauharnais.pdf]].
